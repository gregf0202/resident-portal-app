## Claims to confirm before publishing

Written conservatively, but these are the ones I'd want your eye on. Everything else is either from the fact-checked ledger in `CONTENT_TRADE_PITCH_ARTICLES.md` or verified today.

1. **`17` retention table — QLD row.** I have NSW (7 years, s180) and VIC (7 years, 12 months for voting papers, plus a permanent category) verified. For QLD I've deliberately written *"varies by record — kept under your module"* plus the verified 2-year fire safety installation figure, rather than assert a body corporate retention period I couldn't confirm. If you know the module figure, that row can be firmed up.

2. **`11` resolution thresholds.** I've described ordinary / special / unanimous in words rather than percentages, because the numbers differ by state *and* scheme type and getting one wrong is the fastest way to lose credibility with a strata manager. The doc tells the reader to confirm with their manager before submitting. If you want the actual figures in a table, that's a research job worth doing properly.

3. **`11` notice periods** repeats QLD 21 / NSW, VIC, WA 14. Verified for NSW today (SSMA 2015 Sch 1 cl 7(3)(a) — AGMs 14 days, other general meetings 7). The other three come from your existing `09` table and its sources line.

4. **`03` lot vs common property table** is the general rule plus an honest "ask first" column. Boundaries are set by the registered plan, and the doc says so. Low risk, but it's the table a strata lawyer would read most carefully.

5. **`07` state dispute pathways** — inherited unchanged from your existing Resolving Issues guide, now with a sources line attached. Worth a spot-check that the pathway names are current.

6. **Palette.** Confirm `0B1F3A` navy / `1FA6A6` teal / `5C6670` grey is the real brand set, not the `0C2340` / `0FA3A3` variant. I picked the majority.
